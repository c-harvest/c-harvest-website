About C-Harvest

Family-owned company supporting international sourcing since 1975.

C-Harvest has served clients since 1975, evolving from metal accessory production into a broader sourcing and distribution partner connecting buyers with suppliers across Asia, Latin America, and Europe.

Company Story

From metal accessory production to international sourcing and distribution support.

C-Harvest is a family-owned enterprise with a long operating history and a practical understanding of sourcing, supplier matching, and distribution support for ongoing buyer requirements.

Over time, the company expanded from manufacturing-oriented roots into a broader role that helps international buyers compare suppliers and handle sourcing coordination across regions.

Today, that experience supports clients who need an experienced sourcing contact to connect product demand with supplier capability across regions.

Market Focus

Client Regions

C-Harvest emphasizes support for clients in Asia, Latin America, and Europe, where procurement clarity and supplier access are often important in cross-border sourcing.

Network Objective

The company's role is to establish a useful distribution channel between qualified suppliers worldwide and clients looking for suitable suppliers, workable lead times, and consistent follow-up.
