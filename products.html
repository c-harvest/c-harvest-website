Product Scope

Three product categories currently presented by the company.

C-Harvest currently presents three main product categories: metal parts, pharmaceuticals, and specialty chemicals. This page organizes those categories so buyers can understand the company's sourcing scope more quickly.

01

Metal Parts

A category closely aligned with the company's long-running industrial and manufacturing background.

  • Suitable for industrial sourcing conversations
  • Connects naturally to production heritage
  • Relevant for buyers looking for manufacturing-related sourcing support

02

Pharmaceuticals

Positioned as part of the company's wider sourcing network and supplier access model.

  • Extends the company's sourcing scope beyond metal parts
  • Represents a broader supplier network
  • Supports recurring and new product requests

03

Specialty Chemicals

Presented as a targeted trade domain for clients with more specialized procurement requirements.

  • Covers chemical sourcing requests with more specialized needs
  • Adds a technical product category to the portfolio
  • Supports buyers with more specific procurement requirements

How Product Requests Are Handled

Product requests are handled through requirement review, supplier matching, and delivery coordination.

A

Requirement Review

Product requests start with understanding the item, market, expected quality level, and commercial constraints.

B

Supplier Network Access

The company uses its supplier connections to identify supplier options that match buyer requirements.

C

Commercial And Delivery Follow-Through

After product options are identified, attention shifts to cost, timing, and logistics coordination so the request can move forward.
